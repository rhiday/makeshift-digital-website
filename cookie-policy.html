Cookie Policy

What cookies do, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that websites store in your browser. Similar technologies can also help services understand how a website is used. Some support basic operation and security; others are used for analytics.

Analytics on this website

The website includes Google Analytics to understand visits and page usage, and Microsoft Clarity to understand interactions such as clicks, scrolling and session activity. These services may use cookies or similar identifiers and process device and usage information. For details, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Microsoft’s Privacy Statement.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser’s privacy settings, including cookies already stored for makeshiftdigital.com. Blocking cookies may affect some website features. These controls are managed by your browser; this page does not change your browser settings or record a consent choice.

Additional controls

Google provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on. You can also use your browser’s tracking protection controls. Deleting cookies alone does not prevent new cookies from being stored on a later visit; use blocking settings if that is your preference.

Questions

For questions about cookies or personal information, email hasan@ziplineagency.com or read our Privacy Policy.
