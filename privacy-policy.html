Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are

Makeshift Digital Oy is a digital agency. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit makeshiftdigital.com or contact us. For privacy questions, email hasan@ziplineagency.com.

Information we collect

If you contact us, we may receive your name, email address, company details and any information you choose to share about your project. Our hosting and analytics providers may also process technical information such as your IP address, browser, device, pages visited and interactions with the site.

How we use information

We use enquiry information to respond to you, discuss projects and provide our services. We use technical information to keep the website secure and understand how it is used. Depending on the purpose, our legal basis is taking steps at your request before a contract, performing a contract, meeting legal obligations or our legitimate interest in running our business. Where consent is required for optional analytics, consent is the applicable basis.

Service providers and retention

Our website is hosted on Netlify and includes Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. Clarity can record interactions such as clicks and scrolling. These providers process information under their own terms and may process it outside the European Economic Area. We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including applicable accounting and legal requirements. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, and may have rights to restrict processing, object or receive a copy of your data. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it. Contact hasan@ziplineagency.com to make a request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, including Finland’s Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.

Cookies and updates

Read our Cookie Policy for information about cookies and browser controls. We may update this policy as our website or services change; the date above shows the latest revision.
